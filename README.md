# Mingo Bingo

> Nostalgic May movie challenge bingo card and tracking web app.

🔗 **Live Demo:** [https://fooly-cooly.github.io/Mingo-Bingo/](https://fooly-cooly.github.io/Mingo-Bingo/)

---

## Overview

**Mingo Bingo** is an annual film challenge app designed for the month of May. Players mark off nostalgic, bizarre, and classic films on an interactive 5x5 bingo board.

### Features

- 📅 **Multi-Year Archives:** Includes challenge lists from 2018 through 2023 with carousel navigation.
- 🎯 **Interactive Bingo Card:** Clickable film cells with poster artwork, titles, and release years.
- 🎬 **Trailer & Film Previews:** Integrated modals for reviewing synopses and trailers.
- 💾 **State Persistence:** Remembers watched and checked-off squares locally.
